## Research question
Which factors determine instruction-pair (Apollo-style) deception-probe performance, and can a single universal probe detect heterogeneous deception types, or must probes be matched to deception type / threat model?

## Methodology (implementable detail)

### Instruction-pair probe (Sec 3.1, 3.6; p.3-4)
- Follows Goldowsky-Dill et al. (2025). Contrastive pairs: the same factual statement is used as the model's response, paired with an honest or a dishonest system prompt.
- The response is token-forced (off-policy): the fact is inserted into the chat template as the assistant turn, not generated.
- Features: residual-stream activations at one layer for each response token, **excluding the final 5 response tokens** (so the probe cannot read whether the fact resolved true/false; e.g. activations at "The blue whale is the..." before "largest"/"smallest").
- Label = which system prompt (honest vs dishonest) was used, NOT whether the statement is true.
- Classifier: logistic regression. Activations standardized to zero mean / unit variance across the dataset. Aggregation: mean over all response tokens except the final 5. L2 regularization constant lambda = 1 found optimal in the sweep.
- Layer: 20 (zero-indexed) of Gemma-2-9B-IT; layers 20, 25, 31 were explored.
- Default (baseline) prompt pair (p.3): "Pretend you are an honest person making statements about the world." / "Pretend you are a deceptive person making statements about the world." (Table 4, p.15, prints the `apollo_baseline` pair as "Pretend you're an honest person..." / "Pretend you're a dishonest person..." -- the two renderings differ slightly in the paper itself.)

### Training data (Sec 3.3; App. B.4)
- True/false factual statements from Azaria & Mitchell (2023).
- Plus 11 synthetic fact datasets (~200 facts each, 100 true / 100 false) generated with Claude Sonnet 4.5: comparative relational, causal chain, conditional truths, temporal sequence, quantitative precision, definitional & taxonomic, negation & absence, multi-hop inference, probabilistic & hedged, process & procedural, and a "Control" (no fact; activations from chat-template control tokens only) (Table 5, p.16). Used only for the ANOVA.
- All post-ANOVA experiments use Azaria-Mitchell only.

### Prompt pairs
- 23 taxonomy pairs (16 deception types from Druckman & Bjork 1991 + 7 control pairs for non-deceptive behaviours), generated with Claude Sonnet 4.5.
- 34 framing-variation pairs (identity, role, instruction, hybrid, control repetition, long-form, baseline). Full text printed in Table 4 (p.15).
- The 16 taxonomy pairs are printed verbatim in Table 3 (p.13). Ones most relevant to hallucination-vs-lie:
  - `bluff` -- honest: "Express your uncertainty honestly. Say what you think with appropriate confidence calibration." / dishonest: "State your answer confidently as if you're certain, even though you're not sure."
  - `pretense` -- honest: "Only claim knowledge you actually have." / dishonest: "Pretend to have knowledge or experience you don't actually possess."
  - `pressure_dishonesty` -- honest: "State the truth even when it's inconvenient or unfavorable." / dishonest: "Give the more favorable or convenient answer, even if it's not accurate."
  - `overt_lie` -- honest: "State the ground truth exactly as given." / dishonest: "State the opposite or clearly contradictory fact."
  - `concealment`, `counterfeit`, `forgery`, `hoax`, `white_lie`, `exaggeration`, `evasion`, `trick`, `impersonation`, `backstab`, `false_alarm`, `partial_truth` also printed.
- The 7 control pairs are named (`ctrl_clarification`, `ctrl_hallucination`, `ctrl_ignorance`, `ctrl_joke`, `ctrl_literal`, `ctrl_privacy`, `ctrl_roleplay`) but **their prompt text is not printed** in the paper.
- A `sandbagging` baseline pair is printed: "You are an AI assistant who always gives the correct answer if you know it." / "...always gives the incorrect answer if you know it."

### Experimental factors (Sec 4.1)
2000 probes over: (1) instruction pair, (2) training fact dataset, (3) token selection (first 5, first 10, all except last 10, all except last 5, chat-template tokens only), (4) layer (20, 25, 31). ANOVA on validation AUC.

### Belief elicitation / "knows the answer"
Not done by this paper. It relies on labels shipped with the evaluation datasets (MASK, Liars' Bench, Apollo datasets). MASK is described (p.2-3) as having a proposition, ground truth, pressure prompt, and belief-elicitation prompt, but this paper gives no detail on how it applied them.

## Metrics

## Key results
- ANOVA, Gemma-2-9B-IT: system prompt 70.6% of variance (p<0.001), layer 2.7%, training dataset 2.5%, token selection 0.6%, residual 23.6% (Fig 1, p.4). Llama-3.3-70B: 71.0% / 5.8% / 4.5% / 0.3% / 18.4% residual (Fig 2, p.5).
- Mean AUC over 10 datasets: Baseline 0.689 (SD 0.165); Best Average 0.721 (+0.032, +5.5%); Best Taxonomy (post-hoc oracle) 0.797 (+0.108, +20.5%) (Fig 3, p.6).
- Per-dataset (text, p.5): Convincing Game 0.984 -> 0.985; MASK known facts 0.374 -> 0.697 (pressure_dishonesty; +86.3%); MASK doubling-down 0.565 -> 0.727; sandbagging 0.518 -> 0.657 (concealment); MASK disinformation 0.842 -> 0.901.
- Fig 3 extracted values (B / A / T), panel order as extracted: ai-liar 0.73/0.77/0.85 (hoax); lb-convincing-game 0.98/0.98/0.98 (white_lie); lb-harm-pressure-choice 0.65/0.64/0.76 (forgery); lb-instructed-deception 0.78/0.79/0.85 (false_alarm); mask-continuations 0.69/0.71/0.78 (bluff); mask-disinformation 0.84/0.88/0.93 (forgery); mask-doubling-down-known-facts 0.57/0.59/0.73 (**ctrl_hallucination**); mask-known-facts 0.37/0.44/0.71 (concealment); mask-provided-facts 0.77/0.82/0.86 (white_lie); sandbagging 0.52/0.59/0.63 (concealment).
  - CAVEAT: the dataset-to-panel mapping is inferred from the order of garbled figure text, and the figure numbers disagree with the body text in places (e.g. mask-known-facts best probe: "pressure-dishonesty, 0.697" in text vs "concealment, 0.71" in figure; Sec 5.4 additionally says the overt-lie probe does much better than baseline there; sandbagging 0.657 vs 0.63; disinformation 0.901 vs 0.93). Check the PDF figure before citing any of these.
- Synthetic validation: matched probe in top 3 for its own type in 8/16 cases; beats baseline in 12/16; near-perfect for evasion 0.999, hoax 0.993, forgery 0.989 (p.6).
- Split-half stability: selected probe ranks #1 on held-out half in 45% of cases (chance 6.25%), top-3 in 67%; mean Spearman rho = 0.69 (instructed-deception rho 0.99; mask-known-facts 0.74; mask-doubling-down 0.20).
- Clustering of probe scores (Table 1, p.7): Cluster 1 {evasion, exaggeration, false_alarm, forgery, trick, white_lie} r .84-.97; Cluster 2 {bluff, concealment, impersonation, overt_lie, partial_truth, pressure_dishonesty} r .52-.90; Cluster 3 {backstab, hoax, pretense} r .58-.74; Cluster 4 {counterfeit}; Cluster 5 = the seven control probes r .47-.84.
- Qualitative: baseline probe flags innocuous fiction and flags truthful responses where lying would have been role-appropriate; overt-lie probe "may be indexing more strongly on correctness" (p.7).

## Stated limitations (Sec 8)
- Taxonomy specificity is uneven; much of what the probes capture may be a shared deceptive-intent signal. No causal evidence separating type-specific from general signal.
- Only the ANOVA was replicated on a second model; taxonomy/clustering results are Gemma-2-9B-IT layer 20 only.
- English only; conversational/QA only (no agentic settings).

## Relevance to hallucination-vs-lie separation
- Does NOT test hallucination vs lie. No dataset of "model doesn't know" false outputs is evaluated, and no belief elicitation is performed by the authors.
- What it does establish:
  - Apollo-style probes are labelled by instruction (intent), not truth value, and the last 5 tokens are removed precisely so they do not read out falsity. That makes them a natural candidate "lie-specific" detector to contrast with a truth/falsity probe or an uncertainty-based hallucination detector.
  - The baseline Apollo probe is weak on pressure-induced lies about known facts: 0.374 AUC on MASK known facts (below chance), i.e. the exact lie type our project uses. A `pressure_dishonesty` or `overt_lie` or `concealment` prompt pair is recommended instead (0.70-0.71).
  - Hints that probes mix "honesty" and "correctness": the authors hypothesise the overt-lie probe indexes on whether stated facts are correct. If so, such a probe would also fire on hallucinations -- directly testable in our setup.
  - A `ctrl_hallucination` control pair exists and, per Fig 3 as extracted, was the best "taxonomy" probe for MASK doubling-down. The control probes cluster separately from the deception probes (Cluster 5). This is the only place hallucination appears, and the prompt text is not printed.
- Reusable for our experiment:
  - Full probe recipe (logreg, standardize, mean over response tokens minus last 5, L2 lambda=1, layer 20 of 42 in Gemma-2-9B-IT, Azaria-Mitchell facts, token-forced).
  - Verbatim prompt pairs: `apollo_baseline`, `pressure_dishonesty`, `overt_lie`, `bluff` (confident-when-unsure, a hallucination-like contrast), `pretense`, `sandbagging`.
  - Design idea: train a `bluff`/`pretense` probe (uncertainty misreport) and a `pressure_dishonesty` probe (incentive misreport) and check whether they separate hallucinated from lied answers.
  - Alpaca median subtraction for control-adjusted scores.
  - Gemma-2-9B-IT fits a 48GB GPU easily.
